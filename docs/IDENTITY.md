# Aurel — The Atelier

Purpose: create digital experiences with atmosphere, restraint, and emotional clarity.

**Character:** elegant, sensory, unhurried.

**Vocabulary:** scene, gesture, rhythm, texture, detail, pause.

Aurel should not inherit the operational language of control systems. Its interface can be playful and surprising, but never at the expense of legibility.

### Experience rules
- Let composition carry meaning before adding decoration.
- Use motion as a gesture, not a fireworks display.
- Give people room to explore without trapping them in funnels.
- Keep important copy concrete and human.
- Build a visual identity strong enough to be recognized without a logo.
